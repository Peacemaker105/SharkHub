What this is. A faithful render of the app's screens in its real dark theme — layout, hierarchy and the instrument-cluster type treatment. It is a preview, not the running app. The functional Android project (Kotlin + Compose) is in the delivered Shark Hub zip and builds in Android Studio. Next: polish the visuals — spacing, the tile iconography, the horizon gauge, and a considered accent system for warnings.
